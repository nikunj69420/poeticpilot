What is a cadet programme?

Trained from scratch by the airline's standard.

A cadet pilot programme is an airline-designed pathway that trains aspiring pilots from zero to fully qualified First Officers — with standardised training and a clear airline placement.

01

Eligibility & aptitude

10+2 with Physics and Maths, DGCA medical fitness and strong aptitude.

02

Selection process

Aptitude, psychometric tests, group discussion and personal interview.

03

Flight & ground training

Ab-initio training at an approved academy, following airline SOPs from day one.

04

Airline integration

Type rating and induction into the airline as a First Officer.
