Why it matters

A CPL makes you eligible. A type rating makes you hireable.

Indian airlines operate complex, highly automated transport-category aircraft. A type rating certifies you have completed structured ground school, simulator training and assessment on a specific type — without it, airlines cannot induct you directly into line operations.

The A320 and B737NG are India's most widely flown narrow-bodies, so these two ratings are in constant demand. South Africa offers full-flight simulators, structured training and globally recognised standards suitable for DGCA CPL holders returning to India.

Aircraft options

Pick the cockpit that fits you.

AIRBUS A320

Fly-by-wire, systems-managed

Automation management, protections and ECAM-driven procedures. Ideal for pilots targeting A320 CEO/NEO operators and comfortable in a systems-managed cockpit.

BOEING 737NG

Conventional, hands-on

Manual flying, non-normal handling and checklist discipline with modern avionics. Suited to pilots who prefer direct control and Boeing narrow-body operators.

Questions

Type rating FAQ.

Open to Indian pilots holding a DGCA CPL or frozen ATPL. Fresh CPL holders are welcome; airline experience is not required.

Where is the type rating conducted?

At approved training facilities in South Africa. Poetic Pilot Academy provides complete assistance before and after the training.

Does PPA conduct the simulator training?

No. We assist Indian pilots with preparation, coordination and post-type airline readiness for programmes conducted in South Africa.

Is it suitable for DGCA CPL holders?

Yes — for pilots holding a DGCA CPL or frozen ATPL, with post-type support aligned to DGCA and airline interview expectations.

What does the US$13,000 fee include?

Pre-type rating preparation, type rating training in South Africa, visa assistance, accommodation with breakfast, airport pickup and local transport, VAT/GST, post-type rating support and personal airline interview classes.

Are the interview classes really included?

Yes. One-to-one sessions after your rating, customised to your aircraft type, background and target airline.
