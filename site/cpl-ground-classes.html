Why PPA for CPL

Taught for the exam — and the flight deck.

Success in DGCA exams is built on conceptual clarity, disciplined preparation and continuous mentorship. Our ground school is designed for CPL students preparing alongside flying training or completing ground subjects on their own.

What sets PPA apart is who teaches. Classes are led by active airline pilots flying with leading Indian airlines such as IndiGo and Akasa Air — giving you exposure to real operations and cockpit decision-making while keeping a sharp DGCA focus.

DGCA CPL subjects we cover

Six subjects. One method.

Each subject is taught concept-first, then drilled with DGCA-style questions and full-length mocks.

01 / 06

Air Meteorology

Weather theory, Indian climatology and operational weather decisions.

  • Indian monsoon & seasonal climatology
  • Clouds, turbulence, icing & thunderstorms
  • METAR, TAF, SIGMET & weather charts
  • Weather-related operational risk
02 / 06

Air Regulations

Indian aviation law with exam-oriented interpretation of DGCA rules.

  • DGCA CARs & Indian Aircraft Rules
  • ICAO Annexes relevant to CPL
  • Operational approvals & licensing
  • Exam-focused interpretation
03 / 06

Air Navigation

One of the most scoring subjects — taught step by step with heavy problem practice.

  • Dead reckoning & flight planning
  • Radio navigation & GNSS
  • Time, speed & distance
  • DGCA-style numericals
04 / 06

Technical General

A strong base in systems and aerodynamics for CPL and future airline training.

  • Piston & turbine engines
  • Aircraft instruments & systems
  • Basic jet operations & performance
  • Aerodynamics & principles of flight
05 / 06

RTR (Radio Telephony)

Practical aviation communication with an emphasis on confidence and phraseology.

  • Standard ICAO phraseology
  • RT practical & viva preparation
  • Emergency & abnormal communication
  • Speaking practice
06 / 06

Technical Specific

Aircraft-specific systems training matched to the type you fly.

  • Aircraft type systems
  • Limitations & procedures
  • Performance specifics
  • Exam-pattern practice

Questions

Before you enrol.

Still unsure which subjects to take first? A short call with our team usually settles it.

Who teaches the CPL ground classes?

Active airline pilots flying with leading Indian airlines such as IndiGo and Akasa Air, alongside experienced ground instructors.

Can I join online?

Yes. Live interactive online classes run alongside our Delhi and Bengaluru batches, with recorded lectures and full instructor support.

How long does the course take?

Typically 3 to 6 months, planned around the DGCA examination cycle and your preparation level. Subject-wise enrolment is available.

Can I take classes while doing my flying training?

Yes. The programme is built for both full-time ground students and pilots who are flying at the same time.
