Plan your flying with clarity.

Flying hours are only part of the CPL journey — ground classes, medicals, computer number, DGCA exams, school selection, documentation and conversion all have to line up. We help you plan the whole route.

Why planning matters

Ground and flying are one journey.

Students who treat flying and DGCA theory as separate paths lose months. Plan them together and the CPL arrives faster.

Save time

The right school, country, weather, aircraft availability and paperwork plan can shorten your flying training.

Avoid costly mistakes

Many students start flying without understanding DGCA exams, conversion, medicals or documents — and lose time.
