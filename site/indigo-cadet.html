About the programme

Trained the IndiGo way from day one.

Selected candidates train at DGCA-approved, internationally recognised academies, following IndiGo's standard operating procedures, safety philosophy and operational culture from the very beginning.

Poetic Pilot Academy supports you through aptitude and psychometric preparation, interview readiness, simulator orientation and DGCA documentation.

ELIGIBILITY

Who can apply

  • 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics
  • DGCA Class 1 medical fitness (or eligibility)
  • Strong English communication
  • Ability to clear aptitude, psychometric and interview rounds
Selection process

Nine stages to the flight deck.

A structured, multi-stage process that evaluates aptitude, personality and airline readiness.

01

Choose an academy & apply

Select a preferred flight training organisation; applications depend on each academy's intake.

02

Application screening

Accepted applicants are invited to the initial assessment stages run on IndiGo's behalf.

03

ADAPT assessment

Clear IndiGo's aptitude and psychometric testing.

04

Group discussion

Communication, teamwork and decision-making under observation.

05

Personal interview

Motivation, attitude, airline fit and overall suitability.

06

Join the academy

Successful candidates begin ab-initio flight training at the chosen FTO.

07

Flight training

Complete flying as per the approved syllabus and regulatory requirements.

08

A320 type rating

Airbus A320 type rating to IndiGo's operational standards.

09

Induction

Join IndiGo Airlines as a First Officer.

Frequently asked

IndiGo cadet questions.

What is the IndiGo Cadet Pilot Programme?

A structured, airline-aligned training pathway that prepares aspiring pilots for direct induction into IndiGo as Airbus A320 First Officers.

Who is eligible?

Candidates with 10+2 in Physics and Mathematics who meet DGCA medical standards and clear the aptitude, psychometric and interview assessments.

How much does it cost?

Generally between ₹95 lakh and ₹1.2 crore, depending on the training academy and country.

Is a job guaranteed?

Successful completion leads to induction as a First Officer, subject to meeting all training, performance and regulatory requirements.

Which aircraft will I fly?

Cadets are trained and type-rated on the Airbus A320, IndiGo's primary aircraft.

Can I apply without a Class 1 medical?

You may apply with a DGCA Class 2 medical, but Class 1 certification is mandatory before final induction.

How long does it take?

Typically 18 to 24 months, depending on location, weather and regulatory timelines.
