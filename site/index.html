Who we are

A ground school built around one thing — clarity.

Poetic Pilot Academy is a DGCA-focused aviation academy specialising in CPL ground classes, airline interview preparation and structured pilot training. Every course and practice resource follows the DGCA syllabus and airline selection standards.

From CPL theory to airline-specific preparation like A320 systems, we combine classroom training, mentorship and free no-login practice question banks used by pilot candidates across India.
